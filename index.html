I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at Harvard University, hosted by Ariel Procaccia. My research studies social choice problems (like voting and matching) from the perspective of theoretical computer science.

I received my PhD from Stanford University, advised by Moses Charikar and Li-Yang Tan. I was also an undergrad at Stanford (in math) and had the pleasure of working with Jacob Fox and Mary Wootters.
